A PLACE, NOT A NEW HISTORY

Inside Níhmīwū.

This courtyard is an artistic reconstruction of a small district in the capital, at 500 CE in Terra’s fictional chronology. Its detailed buildings and fountain are newly authored scenery, not features inferred from archaeological evidence or independently simulated events.

Measured surface textures, a captured lighting environment and detailed 3D props replace the previous block-based treatment here. The full planet and its older settlement walks are unchanged. This is a focused visual study, not a claim that the entire world is photorealistic.

No new AI residents have been added to this courtyard. The existing scripted residents remain in the archived settlement walks.
