The source models were repacked as GLB with Meshopt geometry compression. The tree was simplified, its supplied leaf alpha was integrated into the base-color texture, and materials were compressed to WebP. Linen was desaturated for an undyed visual treatment. These modifications do not claim new capture or historical authenticity.
