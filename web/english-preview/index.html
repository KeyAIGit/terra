WHAT THIS WORLD IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

A clearer foundation.

Terra World is a fictional simulation with a playable visual layer.

01

The simulation

The published terra-1 archive supplies the terrain, settlement layouts, aggregate populations, polities, events and notable-person records. The visual rework does not rerun or replace that history.

Read the source ↗
02

The interpretation

Browser architecture, scenery, reconstructed residents and generated portraits make the world easier to explore. They are not proof of independent lives, real historical events or photorealistic game graphics.

The walk deliberately excludes archived people who cannot be placed in its year. Scripted dialogue does not call an AI service.

03

The reference atlas

Terra Atlas is a separate compilation of real-world source datasets. Its millions of rows mostly describe grids, times, places and other structured records. It is not the size of this game's story or population.
